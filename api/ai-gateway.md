# ACME AI Gateway Contract

The AI Gateway is the mandatory control plane between application services and specialist AI agents/providers.

## Pipeline

`request → identity → matter scope → context builder → permitted tools → provider router → provenance → confidence → human gate → audit`

## Specialist boundaries

- Client Intake
- Legal Research
- Case Analysis
- Litigation
- Contract
- Corporate Law
- Tax
- Accounting
- Compliance
- IP
- NCLT
- DRT
- International Law
- Translation
- Document Intelligence
- Evidence
- Conflict Check
- Calendar / Diary
- Billing
- Partner Verification
- Knowledge Acquisition
- Security
- Director Executive AI

## Rule

AI may understand, research, structure, compare, draft and recommend. It must not bypass a required professional human review/approval gate. Provider/model selection is configuration, not hard-coded business logic.
